Make yourself at home.

Orbit: drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan.

Fly (keyboard): W / S forward and back, A / D sideways, E / Q up and down. Hold Shift to go faster, and press + or − to change speed. Drag to look around.

Walk: choose a room, then select Walk inside. Use WASD or arrow keys and your mouse. Escape releases the mouse. On touch devices, drag to look and use the arrow buttons.

Materials: click a surface or choose its group. Select a finish, adjust roughness, and download your schedule.

Reveal interiors removes ceilings and clips the selected level. Room shortcuts also let you reach rooms through closed doors.
